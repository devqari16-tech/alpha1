Who We Are

Our Story

ALPHA Ontario Construction was founded with a single mission: to restore and protect Ontario's buildings with the highest level of craftsmanship and professionalism.

Over the past 14 years, we have grown from a small masonry repair company to a full-service restoration contractor trusted by property managers, building owners, and engineers across Ontario. Our journey has been built on one project at a time, delivered with integrity and attention to detail.

We specialize in the restoration of buildings, parking structures, balconies, and concrete systems. Our team brings together certified tradespeople, restoration engineers, and project managers who are passionate about protecting Ontario's built environment.
